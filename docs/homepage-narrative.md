# Yayin AI Lab - Homepage Narrative

*Note on Visual Direction: Based on recent feedback, we are discarding the "Dark Mode default" from the portfolio, as that was a generic open-source template. The visual treatment recommendations below assume a clean, structured, high-contrast "Lab" aesthetic (light or dual-theme) that feels deliberate, academic, and highly professional.*

---

## 1. Hero
* **Purpose**: Immediately state the company's identity, core offerings, and unique differentiator without relying on generic agency clichés.
* **User Question**: Who are you, what do you actually do, and why should I care?
* **Headline**: Building reliable software. Researching language technology.
* **Supporting Copy**: Yayin AI Lab is a technology engineering team. We build web applications for businesses and educational institutions, provide Microsoft 365 IT enablement, and run an open-source lab focused on preserving Indo-Aryan and Dravidian languages.
* **Primary CTA**: View Portfolio
* **Content/Data Required**: None (pure copy).
* **Visual Treatment**: A stark, academic layout. High-legibility typography (CalSans/Inter). Avoid abstract 3D floating objects. Consider a subtle, technical background element like a schematic grid or an authentic photograph of code/infrastructure.

## 2. Positioning Statement
* **Purpose**: To transition the user from the high-level intro into the dual-track nature of the business (Commercial Services + The Lab).
* **User Question**: Are you a standard dev agency or something else?
* **Headline**: A dual mandate: Commercial engineering and community research.
* **Supporting Copy**: We fund our research by delivering high-quality commercial digital products. When you hire us for web development or IT infrastructure, you get a team with the technical depth of a research lab.
* **Primary CTA**: None.
* **Content/Data Required**: None.
* **Visual Treatment**: Large, oversized typography spanning the width of the page to serve as a visual break.

## 3. Capability Introduction
* **Purpose**: Clearly list the specific, billable services the company offers.
* **User Question**: What can I actually hire you to do?
* **Headline**: Technical capabilities
* **Supporting Copy**: We focus on practical, scalable solutions for small-to-medium businesses and educational organizations.
* **Capabilities List**: 
    1. **Web & App Development**: Custom platforms, informational sites, and conference portals.
    2. **Education Technology**: Institutional software and upcoming open-source SaaS products for schools.
    3. **IT Enablement**: Microsoft 365 licensing, deployment, and support.
* **Primary CTA**: Discuss a project
* **Content/Data Required**: Short descriptions for each capability.
* **Visual Treatment**: A strict 3-column grid. Utilitarian icons with sharp borders. No floating drop-shadow cards; instead, use crisp 1px borders to separate the services, reinforcing the "engineering" feel.

## 4. Selected Work
* **Purpose**: Provide immediate proof that Yayin AI Lab ships real code for real clients.
* **User Question**: What have you actually built?
* **Headline**: Shipped projects
* **Supporting Copy**: Recent platforms, conference websites, and digital products built by our team.
* **Primary CTA**: See all projects
* **Content/Data Required**: High-res screenshots or architecture diagrams from 3-4 key projects (e.g., CBIT conference sites, Wallbee Infra, Rashed Automotives).
* **Visual Treatment**: Large, edge-to-edge imagery for the projects. High-contrast labels showing the tech stack (e.g., `Next.js`, `Hugo`, `Tailwind`) used for each project to appeal to technical stakeholders.

## 5. Lab Initiatives & SaaS
* **Purpose**: Highlight the internal products and research that differentiate the company from a standard agency.
* **User Question**: What is the "Lab" part of your name?
* **Headline**: Inside the Lab
* **Supporting Copy**: Beyond client work, we incubate our own technology. We are currently designing open-source SaaS products aimed at streamlining operations for educational institutes.
* **Primary CTA**: View our GitHub
* **Content/Data Required**: Brief details on the upcoming EdTech SaaS products.
* **Visual Treatment**: Shift the background color slightly (e.g., a very light gray or distinct surface color) to visually separate "The Lab" from the commercial client work.

## 6. Regional Language Preservation Story
* **Purpose**: Detail the most unique cultural differentiator of the company.
* **User Question**: What does language preservation mean in a tech context?
* **Headline**: Engineering for Indo-Aryan and Dravidian languages.
* **Supporting Copy**: The internet defaults to English. We are actively researching and building tools to ensure regional languages remain functional, accessible, and preserved in modern web environments.
* **Primary CTA**: Read our methodology (Optional)
* **Content/Data Required**: 1-2 specific examples of how this preservation is achieved (e.g., specific fonts, translation matrices, or open-source datasets).
* **Visual Treatment**: A typography-focused section. Show actual examples of Dravidian/Indo-Aryan scripts alongside code blocks (using a monospace font) to visualize the intersection of language and technology.

## 7. Technical Credibility / Microsoft Partnership
* **Purpose**: Validate the IT enablement services using the Microsoft partnership.
* **User Question**: Can I trust you with my institutional IT?
* **Headline**: Authorized Microsoft Solutions Partner
* **Supporting Copy**: We provide Microsoft 365 licensing, deployment, and ongoing IT support, tailored specifically for the needs of schools, colleges, and growing businesses.
* **Primary CTA**: None.
* **Content/Data Required**: Official Microsoft Solutions Partner badge.
* **Visual Treatment**: A clean, trust-building band across the screen. Display the Microsoft Partner logo prominently but accurately (do not overstate enterprise AI if it's primarily M365).

## 8. Open-Source/Community Proof
* **Purpose**: Show that the team is active and respected by other developers.
* **User Question**: Are you active in the broader tech community?
* **Headline**: TeamYayin Open Source
* **Supporting Copy**: We don't just consume open-source software; we build it. 
* **Primary CTA**: Explore TeamYayin
* **Content/Data Required**: Real statistics (e.g., number of repositories, contributors, or total users reached - referencing the "25K+ users" if verifiable).
* **Visual Treatment**: Data-heavy. Use raw metrics and terminal-style monospace typography to present GitHub stats directly on the page.

## 9. About Yayin AI Lab & Maker Services
* **Purpose**: Introduce the human element and the hardware/maker side of the business.
* **User Question**: Who runs this, and what else do you do?
* **Headline**: Engineers, designers, and makers.
* **Supporting Copy**: Founded to bridge the gap between high-quality software engineering and cultural impact. When we aren't writing code, we design and build custom mechanical keyboards for the developer community.
* **Primary CTA**: Shop custom keyboards (Links out to Dukaan.io store)
* **Content/Data Required**: A photo of the team or workspace. A high-quality photo of a custom keyboard. Link to the Dukaan.io store.
* **Visual Treatment**: A split layout: one half featuring the team/ethos, the other half showcasing the hardware (keyboards) with a distinct "Shop" button that clearly indicates it goes to an external store.

## 10. Final Call to Action
* **Purpose**: Convert the reader into a lead.
* **User Question**: How do I hire you?
* **Headline**: Start a project with Yayin AI Lab
* **Supporting Copy**: Whether you need a robust web platform for your institution, Microsoft 365 enablement, or a custom digital product, our engineering team is ready to build it.
* **Primary CTA**: Contact us via Email
* **Secondary CTA**: Connect on LinkedIn
* **Content/Data Required**: `mailto:` link, LinkedIn URL.
* **Visual Treatment**: Stark, highly visible block at the bottom of the page. Simple, high-contrast buttons without unnecessary gradients.
