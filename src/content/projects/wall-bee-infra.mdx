We designed website for the Wall Bee Infra Projects. It was established in 2021, Wall Bee has swiftly grown into a reputable company specializing in real estate, residential, and industrial project planning and construction management.

Wall Bee was established with a vision to provide end-to-end solutions in segments such as engineering & construction, real estate, infrastructure, architecture, and structural design. Their approach is meticulously planned, supported by stringent quality practices benchmarked to Indian standards.

The website was developed using the static web framework HUGO and [Educenter theme by Themeforest](https://themefisher.com/hugo-themes/)
